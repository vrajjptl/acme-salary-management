# Demo script

Use this for the short recording the brief asks for. The data is synthetic. Sign-in is `hr.manager` / `Hr-Manager-Demo-2026!`.

1. Open the app and sign in. Mention that the credential is a local demo account.
2. On **How we pay**, point at active headcount, annual base payroll versus total compensation, and the band-compliance split. Say that former employees are excluded from these figures and that groups under five people are hidden in the equity table.
3. Filter the dashboard to one country or one department and show the totals change.
4. Open **People**, search a name, and show that the list is paged rather than loaded at once.
5. Open a person. Show current base against the band, the compa-ratio, and the history.
6. Try a merit increase above 25% with no exception reason. Show the rejection and that history did not change.
7. Submit the same increase with an exception reason, or a normal increase under the cap. Show the new history row and the updated current pay.
8. Optionally terminate nobody on camera if you would rather keep the seed stable. If you do terminate, show that a later pay change is rejected.

That is enough. The interesting part is a rejected change followed by a valid one, not a tour of every filter.
